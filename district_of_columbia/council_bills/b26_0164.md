## Enacted text

```diff
Section 205(f) of the Rental Housing Act of 1985, effective October 22, 2015 (D.C. Law 21-36; D.C. Official Code § 42-3502.05(f)), is amended as follows:
(1) A new paragraph (5) is added to read as follows:
+ “(5)(A) The owner of a rental accommodation that is exempt from rent stabilization pursuant to subsection (a) of this section shall, at the time of registration, provide the following information for each rental unit:
+ (i) The current monthly rent amount;
+ (ii) The monthly rent amount charged for the same unit during each of the two prior calendar years; and
+ (iii) The utilities, facilities and services, and appliances.
+ (B) The information required under subparagraph (A) of this paragraph shall be submitted only at the time of registration and shall not be subject to any ongoing or annual reporting requirements.”
```
